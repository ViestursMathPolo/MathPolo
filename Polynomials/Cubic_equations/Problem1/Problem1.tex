{\bf Problem 1}

Express $\cos\left(3x\right)$ as a function of $\cos\left(x\right)$:

\begin{equation*}
  \cos\left(3x\right)=f\left(\cos\left(x\right)\right);
\end{equation*}

then find $\cos\left(x\right)$ by solving

\begin{equation*}
  \cos\left(3x\right)-f\left(\cos\left(x\right)\right)=0
\end{equation*}

exactly.
